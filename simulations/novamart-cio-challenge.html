Strategic assessment

Debrief question
If another retailer can buy the same technologies tomorrow, what part of your advantage would actually be difficult to copy?

What this simulation was designed to show

  • Technology investments create value as a system, not as isolated purchases.
  • AI becomes more useful when an organization has strong data and analytics capabilities.
  • Cybersecurity is partly an investment decision made before a crisis occurs.
  • Business intelligence connects data to managerial decisions rather than merely producing dashboards.
  • Employee capability and organizational processes can determine whether technology creates value.
  • Competitive advantage depends on combinations of technology, data, people, processes, and execution that may be difficult for rivals to copy.
